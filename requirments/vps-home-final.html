How long does a typical project take?expand_more

A business website usually takes 2 to 4 weeks, a custom web app or mobile app 6 to 12 weeks, and a logo or brand kit 1 to 2 weeks. You get a clear timeline in your quote.

How does pricing work?expand_more

We offer fixed-price packages for defined projects and monthly plans for ongoing work. Every quote lists deliverables and milestones, with no hidden fees.

Do I own the final work?expand_more

Yes. Source code, design files and all accounts are transferred to you in full once the project is completed and paid.

Do you work with clients in other countries?expand_more

Absolutely. We work remotely with clients worldwide and communicate through email, WhatsApp and video calls at times that suit you.

Do you provide support after launch?expand_more

Yes. Every project includes a free support window, and ongoing maintenance plans are available for updates, security and performance.
